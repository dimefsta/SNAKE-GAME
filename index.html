How To Play

🎮 Controls

  • Desktop: Arrow Keys or WASD to steer.
  • Mobile: Swipe anywhere on the board or tap the on-screen D-Pad.
  • Pause / Sound: Press P to pause, M to toggle audio.

🌐 Boundary Modes

  • Classic Mode: Hitting the border wall ends the game immediately.
  • Wrap-around Mode: Slither through walls and re-emerge seamlessly on the opposite side.

⚡ Dynamic Levels & Juice

Every 5 apples eaten speeds up the snake and increases your level. Grab golden bonus apples for +30 extra points!
