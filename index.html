How To Play

🎮 Controls

  • Desktop: Arrow Keys or WASD to steer.
  • Mobile: Swipe anywhere on screen or use the on-screen D-Pad.
  • Pause / Sound: Press P to pause, M to toggle audio.

🌐 Boundary Modes

  • Classic Mode: Hitting the border wall ends the game immediately.
  • Wrap-around Mode: The snake slips through the outer walls and re-emerges on the opposite side.

⚡ Dynamic Levels & Juice

Every 5 food items eaten speeds up the snake and advances your level. Watch out for golden bonus fruits!
